Study Guide 📖✨

🎀 MRS GREN (Characteristics of Life)

  • Movement: Action causing change of position.
  • Respiration: Chemical reactions breaking down nutrients to release energy.
  • Sensitivity: Detecting internal/external stimuli & responding.
  • Growth: Permanent increase in size/dry mass.
  • Reproduction: Producing new offspring (preserving species).
  • Excretion: Removal of metabolic waste & excess substances.
  • Nutrition: Taking in materials for energy & development.

👑 Classification & Naming

Taxa Order: Kingdom ➔ Phylum ➔ Class ➔ Order ➔ Family ➔ Genus ➔ Species

Species: Group that can reproduce fertile offspring.

Binomial System: Genus (Capitalized) + species (lowercase). e.g., Homo sapiens.

⚙️ Cell Organelles

  • Nucleus: Controls activities; contains chromosomes (DNA).
  • Cytoplasm: Site of metabolic chemical reactions.
  • Cell Membrane: Selectively permeable; controls entry/exit.
  • Mitochondria: Site of aerobic respiration (energy release).
  • Ribosomes: Site of protein synthesis.

🐾 Vertebrates Comparison

  • Fish 🐟: Scales, gills, streamline shape, fins.
  • Amphibians 🐸: Moist skin, jelly eggs in water. Tadpoles (gills) ➔ Adults (lungs).
  • Reptiles 🦎: Dry scaly skin, waterproof eggs.
  • Birds 🦅: Feathers, wings, hard eggs, beaks.
  • Mammals 🐄: Hair/fur, Mammary glands (milk), external ear pinna, different teeth.
