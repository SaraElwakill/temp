👑 1. Classification of Living Organisms

5 Major Groups: Animals, Plants, Fungi, Bacteria, & Protista.

⚠️ Viruses are NOT considered living organisms!

Order (KPCOFGS): Kingdom ➔ Phylum ➔ Class ➔ Order ➔ Family ➔ Genus ➔ Species
(King Philip Came Over From Great Spain)

  • Genus🔊: A group of similar species.
  • Species🔊: Group that can reproduce and give fertile offspring.

Binomial System🔊:

Internationally approved Latin names. Genus (Capitalized) + species (lowercase). Must be underlined or italics. Example: Homo sapiens🔊.

Importance: 1. Facilitate identification🔊 2. Facilitate study🔊

🎀 2. Characteristics of Living Organisms (MRS GREN)

  • Movement🔊: An action by an organism causing a change of position or place.
  • Respiration🔊: Chemical reactions in cells that break down nutrient molecules and release energy for metabolism.
  • Sensitivity🔊: Ability to detect/sense stimuli in internal/external environment & make appropriate responses.
  • Growth🔊: Permanent increase in size or dry mass.
  • Reproduction🔊: Produce new offsprings of their own type to preserve the species.
  • Excretion🔊: Removal of waste products of metabolism and substances in excess of requirements.
  • Nutrition🔊: Taking in of materials for energy, growth and development.

🦁 3. Animal Kingdom Features

  • Multicellular🔊 (composed of many cells).
  • No cellulose cell wall & no chloroplasts.
  • Store carbohydrates as Glycogen🔊 in liver & muscle cells.
  • Limited growth until puberty.
  • Move freely.
  • Have nervous coordination🔊 & hormonal coordination🔊.

Classified into 2 main groups: Vertebrates🔊 & Invertebrates🔊

🐾 4. Vertebrates (5 Groups)

1. Fish 🐟🔊

Streamline shape, covered with scales, lateral line of sensation🔊, gills to extract oxygen, bone/cartilage skeleton.

2. Amphibians 🐸🔊 (e.g., Frog, Salamander)

Moist skin with fine capillaries, external ear drums🔊, lay soft jelly-like eggs in water. Tadpoles: external gills & tails. Adults: lungs.

3. Reptiles 🦎🔊 (e.g., Lizards, snakes, crocodile)

Dry scaly skin to reduce water loss, lay waterproof eggs, eardrum inside heads, short/no limbs, body divided into head, neck, trunk & tail.

4. Birds 🦅🔊 (e.g., eagle, owl, hawk)

Covered with feathers (insulating, decrease density, waterproof oil), modified forelimbs into wings, tail for direction, eardrums inside head, lungs, lay hard shell eggs, modified beaks🔊.

5. Mammals 🐄🔊

Body covered with hairy skin or fur, external ear pinna🔊, constant body temp, mammary glands (secrete milk)🔊, different types of teeth, some have whiskers.

🦋 5. Invertebrates & Arthropods (4 Classes)

Invertebrates have NO vertebral column. The largest group is Arthropods. All Arthropods share: 1. Segmented bodies 2. Jointed legs 3. Exoskeleton🔊

1. Insects 🐝🔊 (e.g., bees, silkworm)

3 segments (head, thorax, abdomen). 3 pairs jointed legs. Compound eyes🔊. 1 pair antenna. Wings. Chitin exoskeleton.

2. Arachnids 🕷️🔊 (e.g., spiders, scorpions)

2 segments (cephalothorax, abdomen). 4 pairs jointed legs from cephalothorax ending in claws. No wings. Simple eyes🔊. NO antenna, pair of chelicerae🔊 to hold prey.

3. Crustaceans 🦀🔊

2 segments (cephalothorax, abdomen). More than 4 pairs legs. Respire by gills. TWO pairs antenna. 1 pair stalked compound eyes. Exoskeleton strengthened by calcium salts. No wings.

4. Myriapods 🐛🔊 (e.g., Millipede, Centipede)

Body divided into head & identical abdominal segments. 1 pair of legs per segment. Fused ventral segments make it look like 2 pairs. 1 pair antenna. Grows by adding segments.

⚙️ 6. Cell Structure & Organisation

The basic unit of life is the cell. Unicellular🔊 (1 cell) vs Multicellular🔊 (>1 cell).

Common Organelles (Animal & Plant) 🤝

  • Nucleus🔊: Controls all cell activities & starts division. Contains chromosomes🔊 (made of DNA with genetic codes).
  • Cytoplasm🔊: Jelly-like substance where metabolic chemical reactions take place. Stores food/wastes.
  • Cell surface membrane🔊: Proteins & lipids. Selectively permeable🔊; controls molecules in/out.
  • Mitochondria🔊: Have enzymes. Site of aerobic respiration🔊 and energy release.
  • Ribosomes🔊 & Rough ER: Site of protein synthesis🔊. Ribosomes attach to the wall of Endoplasmic Reticulum.

Plant Cell ONLY 🌿

  • Large Central Sap Vacuole🔊: Space surrounded by membrane; acts as sugar/water store. (Animals have small vacuoles).
  • Cell Wall🔊: Rigid structure made of cellulose🔊. Fully freely permeable🔊. Supports/protects, prevents bursting, maintains turgor pressure.
  • Chloroplasts🔊: Contain chlorophyll pigments to trap sunlight for photosynthesis. Stores starch granules.
